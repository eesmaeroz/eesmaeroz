Tomato disease detection system built at the **Google AI Technology Academy** hackathon.  
YOLOv8 + EfficientNet-B4 models, deployed on Railway and integrated into a Flutter mobile app.

### 🚶 [RouteWalk](https://github.com/eesmaeroz/routewalk)
Flutter & Firebase walking-route app with GPS routing (OSRM), live activity tracking,  
AI-generated route stories with the Gemini API, and social features.

### 🚗 AutoTrack AI *(in progress)*
4-person team building an autonomous vehicle on **NVIDIA Jetson** that handles lane following,  
traffic lights, overtaking, pedestrian crossings and parking on a test track.  
**My role:** Hardware & vehicle control.

Hackathon project: an AI assistant that listens to your mood, built with n8n automation and Google Gemini.

HTML cleaning, spell correction (rapidfuzz, symspellpy), multi-word term preservation and language detection, all covered by unit tests.
